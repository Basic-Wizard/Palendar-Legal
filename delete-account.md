# Delete your Palendar account

This page explains how to delete your account in **Palendar**, the social calendar app by Basic Wizard Quorum, and what happens to your data when you do.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open Palendar and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap the **menu icon (☰)** in the top-right corner to open Settings, then tap **Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and follow the steps.

You'll be asked to:

* pick a new owner for any group you own that has other members,
* choose whether to delete or hand over any upcoming event you host that other people are going to, and
* re-enter your password to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately once you confirm.

## Don't have the app anymore?

Email **privacy@basicwizardquorum.com** from the email address you signed up with, with the subject **"Delete my Palendar account"**. Include your Palendar username if you remember it.

We'll confirm the request came from you, delete the account within **7 days**, and email you when it's done.

## What gets deleted

* Your profile (name, username, email, and calendar color) and your login
* Your friends list, friend requests, calendar shares, and blocked users
* Your availability and unavailable times
* Events you host, unless you hand them over to someone else first
* Your RSVPs, items you said you'd bring, and your notification settings and device push tokens
* The text of your chat messages. In shared group and event chats, each one is replaced with "Message from a deleted user" so the conversation still makes sense.
* Reports you've filed about other people

## What we keep, and why

* **Expenses you paid in other people's events.** The amount and description stay so the event's total is still correct for everyone else. They show as paid by "Deleted user" and are no longer linked to you.
* **Groups and events you handed over.** These belong to their new owner.
* **Reports other people filed about you,** including a copy of any reported message. We keep these for up to **2 years** to keep Palendar safe and to deal with repeat abuse. They're only seen by the Palendar team.
* **Backups.** Copies of deleted data may remain in routine database backups for up to **30 days** before they're overwritten.

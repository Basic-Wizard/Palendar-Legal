# Palendar Child Safety Standards

**Effective: October 2026**

Palendar, a social calendar app for planning events with friends and groups, is made by Basic Wizard Quorum ("we", "us", "our"). This page explains how we protect children and what we do about child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

## Our standards

We have **zero tolerance** for child sexual abuse and exploitation. The following are strictly prohibited anywhere in Palendar, including event and group titles, descriptions, messages, and profiles:

* Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind
* Sexual or sexualized content involving minors
* Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to contact a minor for sexual purposes
* Sextortion, or threatening or coercing anyone with sexual content
* Any other content or behavior that sexually exploits or endangers children

These rules are also part of our [Terms of Use](terms.html) (section 3). Palendar is not intended for children under 13, and users under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission.

## How to report

You can report users and messages from inside the app. Open the person's profile or the message and use **Report**. You can also **block** any user at any time. Reports about child safety can also be sent by email to **privacy@basicwizardquorum.com**.

## What we do about reports

* We review reports **within 24 hours**.
* We remove content that violates these standards and **permanently delete the accounts** responsible.
* We keep the reported content and related account information as needed for safety and legal purposes.
* We **report CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** and to other appropriate authorities as required by law, and we cooperate with law enforcement requests.
* We may act on content we find ourselves, not only on reports.

## Child safety contact

Questions, reports, or concerns about child safety: **privacy@basicwizardquorum.com**.

We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including those related to reporting CSAM.
